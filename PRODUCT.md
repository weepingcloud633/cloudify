# Product

**Cloudify** is a Windows desktop music player by weepingcloud: search YouTube Music, play instantly from a local cache, keep playlists, import and export them. A Rust rewrite of Silva Music, built to start fast and stay light.

## Register
Product (Operate mode): an app used for hours. Scanability and responsiveness outrank expression; brand lives in details.

## Users
Listeners who keep their music in playlists and move between services (Spotify exports, YouTube links, friends' share codes).

## Visual direction
Material 3 Expressive, taken from Swiftify (GPL-3.0): tonal surfaces coloured from the playing album art, scalloped and wavy shapes, a floating pill player bar, a liquid blurred backdrop in the immersive player. Dark first.

## Principles
1. Music first: the player bar is always reachable; nothing blocks playback.
2. Colour follows the cover: the whole palette is derived from the current album art.
3. Motion answers input and never loops without cause; under 250 ms, ease-out.
4. Light and quick: no web engine, few moving parts.

## Assumptions (no interview was possible)
- Dark theme only for now. English UI only. Windows 11 only.
- Lyrics, hotkeys, downloads and profiles come after the redesign.
